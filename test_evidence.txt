CYBERSECURITY POLICY DOCUMENT
Organization: Test Company
Date: January 15, 2026

GOVERNANCE STRUCTURE (GV.OC)

Our organization has established a comprehensive cybersecurity governance program aligned with the NIST Cybersecurity Framework. This document outlines our key governance and oversight mechanisms.

1. ORGANIZATIONAL CONTEXT (GV.OC-01)
The organization's cybersecurity objectives are clearly defined and communicated to all stakeholders. We maintain an understanding of how cybersecurity risks relate to our business objectives and strategic priorities.

2. RISK MANAGEMENT STRATEGY (GV.RM)
We have implemented a risk management strategy (GV.RM-01) that guides our approach to identifying, assessing, and responding to cybersecurity risks. This strategy is reviewed annually and updated based on changes in the threat landscape and business environment.

3. ROLES AND RESPONSIBILITIES (GV.OC-02)
Cybersecurity roles and responsibilities are clearly defined across all organizational levels. The CISO reports directly to the CEO and has authority to implement security controls.

ASSET MANAGEMENT (ID.AM)

4. ASSET INVENTORY (ID.AM-01)
We maintain a comprehensive inventory of all physical devices and systems within our organization. This inventory is updated quarterly and includes:
- Servers and network equipment
- Workstations and laptops
- Mobile devices
- IoT devices
- Critical infrastructure components

All assets are tagged with unique identifiers and tracked in our CMDB system.

5. SOFTWARE PLATFORM INVENTORY (ID.AM-02)
Software platforms and applications are inventoried with version information, license details, and business owners assigned. We track both commercial and internally developed applications.

IDENTITY MANAGEMENT AND ACCESS CONTROL (PR.AC)

6. ACCESS CONTROL POLICIES (PR.AC-01)
Identities and credentials are issued, managed, verified, revoked, and audited for authorized devices, users, and processes. We implement:
- Multi-factor authentication (MFA) for all privileged accounts
- Role-based access control (RBAC)
- Regular access reviews conducted quarterly
- Automated deprovisioning for terminated employees

7. PHYSICAL ACCESS CONTROLS (PR.AC-02)
Physical access to assets is managed and protected through badge systems, security cameras, and visitor management protocols.

CONTINUOUS MONITORING (DE.CM)

8. NETWORK MONITORING (DE.CM-01)
Networks and network services are monitored to find potentially adverse events. We utilize:
- SIEM system for security event correlation
- IDS/IPS for intrusion detection and prevention
- Network traffic analysis tools
- 24/7 SOC monitoring

9. ANOMALY DETECTION (DE.CM-03)
Personnel activity and technology usage are monitored to find potentially adverse events. We implement user behavior analytics (UBA) to detect anomalous activities.

INCIDENT RESPONSE (RS.AN)

10. INCIDENT ANALYSIS (RS.AN-01)
Notifications from detection systems are investigated and analyzed to ensure effective response. Our incident response team follows a documented playbook for categorizing and prioritizing incidents.

11. INCIDENT IMPACT ANALYSIS (RS.AN-02)
The impact of incidents is understood, and incident data is used to improve our security posture.

RECOVERY PLANNING (RC.RP)

12. RECOVERY PROCEDURES (RC.RP-01)
Recovery processes and procedures are executed and maintained to ensure restoration of systems and services affected by cybersecurity incidents. We conduct quarterly disaster recovery tests.

This policy is approved by executive leadership and reviewed annually.
